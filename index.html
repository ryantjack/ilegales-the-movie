Overview

A human story behind border headlines.

ILEGALES explores why people migrate: to work, provide, flee danger, and seek a better life. It frames immigration through dignity, risk, and the personal cost of impossible choices.

To Work

Immigrants are shown within labor systems that rely on them, especially low-skilled and agricultural work.

To Provide

The film highlights families supported by relatives who have left home to earn money elsewhere.

To Flee

Violence, poverty, fear, and instability create pressure that makes migration feel like survival.

Synopsis

Five connected stories in the desert.

Headlights flicker down a desert trail. “La Migra! Vámonos!” In the chaos, dreams scatter with the people carrying them.

Maribel Chacón

A waitress in Juarez hopes to become a U.S. citizen, but her son's urgent medical needs force her toward a dangerous decision.

Ariel

A day laborer in New Mexico slowly faces darker choices as necessity collides with his convictions.

Arturo Chacón

A farm worker with moral clarity must decide where his loyalties belong when immigration enforcement closes in.

Ignacio Rivera

A single father working in a diner lives under the threat of discovery while agents see him as a path to a cartel.

Chuy Sanchez

A low-level coyote and trafficker drags others into danger while searching for his own escape.

The Question

Through heartbreak and hope, the film asks: what lines would you cross for a better life?
